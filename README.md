# Bread Clicker
A simple 2D clicker game made in Godot 4. Click the bread, buy upgrades, and rebirth to get stackable discounts.


# Where to play?
Play the demo on itch.io: https://mubixd.itch.io/

## Game Mechanics

- **Minigame:** Win the minigame to gain +20% more bread. If you die you lose 20% of your bread.
- **Rebirth:** Resets your current clicks and upgrade levels in exchange for a permanent 10% discount on item prices (up to 9 times).
- **Falling Goodies:** Catch either coffee beans or croissants to gain boosts! Coffee beans give you a certain amount of bread based on how many upgrades you are and croissants gives you 2x bread for a certain time if collected


## Shop Upgrades

- **Baguette:** Boosts your bread per click (BPC).
- **Tea:** +1 bread per second (BPS).
- **Espresso:** +5 bread per second.
- **Matcha:** +10 bread per second.


## Tutorial

- Click the bread to get bread.
- Spend the bread inside the shop (on the right) to get even more bread!

## Tools Used
Godot and a little bit of VSCODE
